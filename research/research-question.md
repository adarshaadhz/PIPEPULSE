PIPEPULSE — Research Question

Core Research Question

Can two low-cost acoustic sensors, combined with signal processing and machine learning, reliably distinguish water pipeline leaks from normal operating noise under controlled experimental conditions?

Research Objectives

1. Record acoustic and vibration signals from a test pipeline.
2. Compare signals under leaking and non-leaking conditions.
3. Investigate how flow rate and background noise affect detection.
4. Compare basic signal-processing methods with machine-learning models.
5. Explore whether two sensors can estimate the approximate leak location.

Initial Hypothesis

Combining measurements from two sensors may improve leak detection compared with relying on a single sensor.

This hypothesis must be tested experimentally.

Current Research Stage

Literature review and experimental planning.

No prototype results have been established yet.

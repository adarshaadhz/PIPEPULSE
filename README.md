PIPEPULSE

Affordable acoustic technology for experimental water pipeline leak detection.

Overview

PIPEPULSE is an early-stage deep-tech research project exploring whether low-cost acoustic sensors and signal processing can detect leaks in water pipelines.

The aim is to investigate an accessible way to identify leaks before they cause significant water loss.

Research Objectives

• Capture acoustic and vibration signals from pipelines.
• Distinguish leak signals from normal operating noise.
• Explore signal processing and machine learning.
• Investigate leak-location estimation using two sensors.
• Evaluate performance under different conditions.

Technology

• Acoustic and vibration sensors
• Python signal processing
• Machine learning
• ESP32-S3 (planned)

Current Status

Research and planning stage. Hardware development and controlled experiments are planned. No experimental performance has been established yet.

Next Steps

1. Build a controlled pipeline test rig.
2. Collect labelled acoustic data.
3. Develop and test detection algorithms.
4. Evaluate results and document limitations.

Vision

Explore a low-cost, practical and reproducible approach to water pipeline leak detection.
